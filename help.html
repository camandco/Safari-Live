How to use Safari Live

Our trip app for ticking off every animal and bird we see, even with no signal. Five minutes to set up, then just tap.

Open the appSee the dashboard
On this page 1. Set it up on your iPhone 2. Log a sighting 3. No signal? No problem 4. The dashboard 5. The menu 6. If something goes wrong

1Set it up on your iPhone

Do this at home, with Wi-Fi or signal, before we leave.

  1. Open camandco.github.io/Safari-Live/tick.html in Safari, not Chrome or WhatsApp's browser.
  2. Tap Share (the square with the arrow) and choose Add to Home Screen. If you see Open as Web App, leave it switched on. Tap Add.
  3. Close Safari. Open Safari Live from the new giraffe icon on your home screen.
  4. Enter the trip password (Cam will send it to you) and tap Enter.
  5. Tap your name. Everything you log on this phone is saved under your name.
  6. Tick something easy to test it. When your phone asks to use your location, tap Allow While Using App. Then tap Undo on the message at the bottom to remove the test.
  7. Go to iPhone Settings › Mobile Data and make sure Safari is switched on, so the app can sync on mobile data.
Always use the giraffe iconOpening the link in Safari is a separate copy with its own login and its own unsynced sightings. Stick to the home screen icon.

2Log a sighting

Takes about five seconds.

  1. Open Log sightings from the giraffe icon.
  2. Pick the park at the top: Kruger or Pilanesberg.
  3. Search, or scroll. The list starts with the most common animals. Use the filters for Mammals, Birds, Not seen yet or Big Five.
  4. Tap Seen it next to the animal. Wait a second for 📍 Location found, then tap Seen it again.
  5. Done. The time, your name, the park and the GPS spot are all saved for you.
First to spot it winsEach species can only be ticked once per park. If it already shows a ✓ with someone's name, they got there first.
Ticked the wrong one?Tap Undo on the message at the bottom straight away. Once it has synced, ask Cam to untick it in the sheet.

3No signal? No problem

Most of Kruger's roads have no signal. The app is built for that.

You seeWhat it means
No signal, saving on this phoneKeep ticking. Everything is saved on your phone, GPS included.
3 waiting to syncThree sightings are still only on your phone. They send by themselves once you have signal.
⏳ waiting to sync (on a species)You ticked it, but it hasn't reached the group yet.
3 sightings synced to the groupThey're safely in the shared sheet. Nothing can be lost now.
✓ Name · time (on a species)It's in the sheet, ticked by that person.
The golden ruleWhen you get back to camp, open the app for a moment. Once there's no "waiting" number at the top, everything is safe. You don't need Wi-Fi: one bar of mobile signal is enough.
Don't delete the app while sightings are waitingForce closing, restarting or flight mode is fine. Deleting the app or clearing Safari data before it syncs will lose those sightings.

6If something goes wrong

ProblemTry this
No 📍 location on sightingsiPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Safari Websites › While Using.
Sightings won't syncCheck you have signal and Safari has mobile data, then tap Sync. The message at the top says what's wrong.
"Trip password is wrong"Open the menu, tap Log out and sign in again with the right password.
Logged in as the wrong personMenu › Switch person.
Dashboard says it can't reach the sheetYou have no signal. It tries again by itself.
Something looks old after an updateFully close the app and reopen it. The version number is at the bottom of Log sightings.
